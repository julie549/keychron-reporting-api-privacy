Privacy Policy for Keychron Reporting API

App ID: 2026853651274461 Effective date: October 13, 2026 Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shen Zhen Shi Bi Pu Ke Ji You Xian Gong Si, located at Room 201, Building 2, Wenkeng Industrial Park, No. 24 Dafa Road, Dafapu Community, Bantian Street, Longgang District, Shenzhen, Guangdong, China ("we," "us," or "our"), processes information through the Keychron Reporting API (the "Service").

The Service is an internal advertising-management tool used only by authorized personnel and service providers to read Meta advertising data, administer authorized advertising accounts and product catalogs, and create or manage advertising objects through the Meta Marketing API. It is not a consumer-facing application and does not provide public user registration.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to information processed by the Service in connection with authorized Meta business assets, including Meta ad accounts, Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts used for advertising, Pixels or Datasets, and product catalogs.

It does not replace Meta's own privacy notices. Information retained by Meta is governed by Meta's terms and privacy policies.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the action requested by an authorized operator, the Service may process:

  • Authentication and permission information: a Meta access token, token-subject or system-user identifiers, permission status, and authorization errors. The access token is used only to authenticate requests to Meta.
  • Business and asset information: business, ad-account, catalog, Product Set, Pixel or Dataset, Facebook Page, and Instagram account identifiers; asset names; account status; currency; balance; spend; and related configuration metadata.
  • Advertising configuration: campaign, ad set, ad, and creative identifiers and names; objectives; budgets; bid settings; schedules; delivery status; targeting specifications; attribution settings; tracking specifications; promoted objects; destination URLs; and URL tags.
  • Advertising performance information: aggregated or Meta-provided metrics such as impressions, reach, clicks, spend, actions, conversions, cost metrics, and aggregated breakdowns by dimensions such as geography, age range, gender, device, placement, or time.
  • Catalog and product information: product and retailer identifiers; product names; Product Set membership and filters; availability and inventory; review and visibility status; price and sale price; product URLs; image URLs; and product-group relationships.
  • Creative and media information: advertising copy, headlines, descriptions, calls to action, images, videos, thumbnails, media hashes, post or story specifications, Page and Instagram identity references, and preview links. Creative media may incidentally include identifiable individuals.
  • Activity and administration information: change-history entries, timestamps, object names and identifiers, application identifiers, and the names or identifiers of authorized administrators or system users who performed account actions.
  • Technical information: API request parameters, API responses, pagination cursors, error codes, request status, and short-lived confirmation records used to verify approved write operations.
  • Operator-provided instructions and files: information that an authorized operator submits to the Service, including campaign instructions and advertising media. If the operator provides a public media URL, the Service retrieves the media from that URL before transmitting it to Meta.

Based on the implementation audited as of the effective date, the Service does not provide Facebook Login, request personal Meta login credentials, retrieve private messages, upload customer email or phone lists, or retrieve individual-level identities from aggregated advertising metrics. The Service does not use the App's permissions to read Instagram comments, retrieve Instagram Insights, publish Instagram content, or read Page user content. No other internal tool uses those permissions under this App ID.

3. Sources of Information

We receive information from:

  • Meta through the Meta Graph API and Marketing API;
  • authorized personnel who instruct the Service or provide advertising assets; and
  • public media URLs selected by an authorized operator.

4. How We Use Information

We process information only for authorized business purposes, including to:

  • authenticate the Service and verify permissions;
  • retrieve and analyze advertising-account, campaign, creative, performance, Page, and catalog information;
  • prepare internal advertising reports and operational reviews;
  • create, update, pause, resume, schedule, or otherwise manage authorized campaigns, ad sets, ads, creatives, media assets, and Product Sets;
  • validate proposed changes and obtain operator confirmation before sensitive write operations;
  • troubleshoot API errors, protect credentials, monitor authorized account activity, and maintain the security and reliability of the Service; and
  • comply with applicable law, contractual obligations, and Meta platform requirements.

We do not sell Meta API data. We do not use Meta API data to build independent profiles of individual Meta users.

5. Legal Bases

We process information only where we have an appropriate basis under applicable law, including as necessary to provide authorized advertising-management services, based on valid authorization or consent where required, and to comply with legal obligations.

6. How Information Is Shared

We disclose information only as necessary for the purposes described above:

  • Meta: information is exchanged with Meta Platforms, Inc. and/or the applicable Meta affiliate through Meta's APIs to provide advertising and catalog functionality.
  • OpenAI / Codex: OpenAI Codex is used under a Pro subscription as an AI programming and automation tool in the authorized advertising-management workflow. Authorized operators provide instructions through Codex; Codex sends scoped tool requests to the local MCP server, and the server returns relevant Meta API results to Codex for analysis, proposed actions, and responses. Commands and MCP requests execute on the authorized local computer, while task context may be processed by OpenAI systems. OpenAI's processing, retention, and deletion of Codex task content are governed by the settings, terms, and policies applicable to the Pro subscription; we do not represent that Business or Enterprise controls apply.
  • GitHub: GitHub Pages hosts this public Privacy Policy. Advertising-account data and Meta access tokens are not intended to be placed in the policy repository. GitHub may process ordinary website-access information for visitors under its own privacy terms.
  • Public media hosts: when an authorized operator provides an external image URL, the host of that URL receives an ordinary request for the selected file and may receive standard connection metadata.
  • Other vendors: the audited deployment does not use an additional cloud host, proxy, monitoring provider, logging provider, backup service, or external consultant with access to Service data.
  • Legal and safety disclosures: we may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, security, or the integrity of the Service and authorized Meta assets.

Each third-party service processes information under its applicable terms and privacy policies.

7. International Transfers

Meta, OpenAI, GitHub, and public media hosts may process information in countries other than China or the country in which an authorized operator or affected individual is located. We take steps required by applicable law when information is transferred internationally.

8. Storage and Retention

The audited MCP server does not contain a database and does not write Meta API responses or advertising data to persistent storage by itself. It:

  • holds the Meta access token in process memory while running;
  • transmits requests to Meta over HTTPS;
  • returns API responses to the authorized MCP client;
  • removes access tokens from returned pagination URLs; and
  • stores only a cryptographic hash, operation name, and expiration time for a write-confirmation record in memory for up to 15 minutes.

The server writes startup and operational error messages to standard error but does not intentionally log access tokens or complete advertising datasets.

Information may nevertheless be retained outside the MCP server in authorized Codex task history, local workspaces, or generated reports. We retain such information only for as long as necessary to carry out authorized advertising-management purposes, unless a longer period is required by law or necessary to resolve security, compliance, or legal matters. Credential storage outside the MCP process is controlled by the authorized local environment. Credentials are retained only for as long as required to operate the Service and are revoked or replaced when no longer needed.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. These measures include access controls, system-user permissions, HTTPS transmission to Meta, restricted internal use, confirmation controls for sensitive write operations, token redaction in pagination results, and limiting writes to authorized account assets. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Data Deletion Instructions and Privacy Requests

The Service does not create public user accounts. To request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other applicable privacy rights concerning information controlled by us, email julie@keychron.cn with the subject line "Keychron Reporting API Privacy Request."

Please identify the Meta business asset, Page, Instagram account, advertising account, or other context relevant to the request. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify identity, authority, and the scope of the request.

After a verified deletion request, we will delete or de-identify information in systems under our control unless retention is required or permitted by law, security needs, dispute resolution, or another valid legal basis. We will also instruct applicable service providers to delete information where required. We will process the request within the period required by applicable law.

Because the audited MCP server does not persist Meta API responses, closing the relevant session removes its in-memory operational data, and write-confirmation records expire automatically within 15 minutes. Deleting information from local reports, Codex task history, backups, or other approved systems follows the confirmed organizational deletion process described above.

Requests concerning information retained by Meta must also be directed to Meta through Meta's privacy controls. Removing access to the Service or revoking the system-user token prevents future retrieval but does not itself delete information retained in Meta's systems.
